Malak Al Tawouk

A triple-blind neuro taste test conducted to measure emotional responses to the brand’s signature sandwich, stripped from branding influence.

Technologies Used:

Conducted a triple-blind comparison with competitors.

Used EEG to measure engagement, attention, and emotional intensity.

Aimed to validate if the product itself lived up to the brand equity.

Recommendations:

Competitor’s sandwich ranked higher in blind preference.

Brand equity remained strong, but product experience lagged.

Provided R&D recommendations to improve flavor, texture, and satisfaction.
