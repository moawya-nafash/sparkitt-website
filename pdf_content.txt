neurotests 	process
1. Discovery Workshop: Understand the client’s objectives,
business challenge, target audience, and object of testing.
2. Test Design: Build the neurotesting protocol, stimulus
structure, testing flow, and measurement framework.
3. Neuro-Sampling: Define and recruit the right participants
based on the client’s target audience and customer segments.
4. Implementation Setup: Choose between two execution
models: SparKitt field deployment or remote client-led testing with
SparKitt supervision.
5. Neurotechnology Testing: Collect data using EEG, Meta
glasses, neuro-mobile platforms, eye-tracking, and predictive
fMRI.
6. Data Cleaning: Validate, clean, synchronize, and prepare all
neuro, visual, and behavioral data.
7. Data Modeling: Use AI, machine learning, and statistical
analysis to detect attention, emotion, friction, and engagement
patterns.
8. Neuroscientific Explanation: Explain the results through
neuroscience, psychology, and behavioral science.
9. Recommendations: Translate insights into strategic,
creative, communication, and experience improvements.
10. Final Report & Presentation: Deliver and present the final
report with clear findings, implications, and action-oriented
recommendations.
The neurotesting process ends with the delivery of the final report. However, when the project requires strategy development,
the findings become the strategic inputs on which we build the brand, marketing, or multisensory strategy.

Neuromarketing?
The science of understanding how consumers truly feel, think, and
decide, beyond what they say. Neuromarketing uses neuroscience
and biometric tools to explore the subconscious drivers behind
choices. It reveals the emotions and reactions that traditional
research methods often miss.
By measuring brain activity, attention, and emotional response,
neuromarketing provides insights into what truly influences decision-
making.
It is not about what consumers claim to prefer.
It is about uncovering what they respond to.
Neuromarketing brings science into strategy, helping brands connect,
persuade, and grow with precision.

AI-based Eye-tracking is a neurotechnology that
predicts where people will look first in any visual
design within just 3 to 5 seconds.
Unlike traditional Eye-tracking that requires physical
devices and human testing, this solution uses deep
learning models trained on over 5.5 million real eye
movements to simulate human vision with up to 96%
accuracy.
It shows exactly which parts of a post, ad, or visual
draw attention and which get ignored.
That means you can test your content before
publishing, ensuring your brand, message, and call-to-
action are seen clearly, instantly, and in the right
order.

3- 	FMRI mapping
Predictive fMRI is an advanced neuroscience modeling tool
that helps estimate how the brain is most likely responding
at the cortical level, based on measured neural and
physiological signals.
Unlike traditional fMRI, which requires a full imaging setup
inside a scanner, Predictive fMRI uses AI-driven modeling
to translate collected neuro-data into probable brain-
response maps. This allows us to understand not only that
a person reacted, but also what kind of neural-state
pattern was most likely activated.
We use Predictive fMRI to add a deeper layer of
neurological interpretation, helping reveal whether the
measured response reflects:
• 	stable emotional regulation
• 	cognitive readiness
• 	calming and downshifting
• 	immersive mental flow
• 	recovery-oriented brain states
• 	or other objective-specific neural patterns

Brand Psychology
This pillar is rooted in the methodology of Brands on the Therapy Couch,
our book that approaches branding through the lens of psychology, not
just marketing.
At its core is the BranDisorders Wheel, a diagnostic tool that identifies
20 distinct disorders. These help uncover what is holding a brand back,
from identity confusion to emotional disconnection or audience
misalignment.
To address these challenges, the AntiBrandiotics methodology was
developed. It is a structured framework designed to prevent, treat, or
reverse brand disorders through psychologically grounded strategies.
At SparKitt, this approach ensures that every branding and rebranding
process begins with a clear psychological diagnosis and leads to a
strategy that is emotionally intelligent, behaviorally aligned, and
scientifically sound.

a faster, clearer, and more meaningful interpretation of the
world.

In practical terms, people remember what they
sense as one coherent experience.

official song clip
You Speak World wanted to understand how its video

clarity, engagement, and platform-fit performance.

Not every strong visual
creates the same response.

with the same precision.
This project tested the You Speak World video across

final attention, EEG, visual rhythm, and predicted fMRI

What emerged was a clear, engaging, and neuro-

METHODOLOGY
We conducted a multi-layer neuro evaluation of the video using on-location eye-
tracking, editing-stage testing, final video attention analysis, EEG, frame-to-frame
visual similarity, and predictive fMRI modeling.
SCIENTIFIC INSIGHT
The findings showed that the YSW video performs as a strong and well-balanced
stimulus. Eye-tracking confirmed effective attention guidance, especially in the social
version. EEG showed strong attention, interest, and cognitive engagement with a
moderate, positive emotional response. Visual rhythm analysis and predicted fMRI
both indicated a stable and sustained engagement pattern.
STRATEGIC OUTCOME
We translated these findings into a clearer creative framework for video optimization.
The project confirmed that YSW’s performance comes from strong attention
guidance, visual hierarchy, emotional readability, and platform-fit execution, making
the content more memorable, effective, and scalable.

NEURO EVALUATION OF
FUNCTIONAL AUDIO CHANNELS
MawjApp in Jordan wanted to validate what each audio
channel actually does in the brain, and position everyone
with greater scientific precision.

-- 38 of 75 --

Not every calming sound
creates the same state.
Not every activation sound
drives the same outcome.
This project tested a six-channel functional audio
suite designed to support different mental and
emotional goals.
What emerged was not one generic “audio
effect,” but a well-differentiated system of
distinct neural outcomes across recovery, focus
preparation, nap support, sleep support, resonant

-- 39 of 75 --

METHODOLOGY
We conducted a neuro study with 6 participants across MawjApp’s audio suite. Each
participant listened to 6 different channels. The study combined EEG and predictive
fMRI enrichment to evaluate how each channel shaped attention, engagement,
relaxation, stress, and overall mental-state response.
SCIENTIFIC INSIGHT
The findings showed that the six channels did not create a repeated audio effect.
Instead, each one produced a distinct and interpretable mental-state profile.
Emotional Recovery, Focus, Sleep, Flowstate, Motivation, and Power Nap each aligned
with a different neurological pattern, allowing the suite to be understood as a
differentiated system rather than a single generalized experience.
STRATEGIC OUTCOME
We translated these findings into a clearer portfolio strategy by defining each
channel according to the mental-state effect it most consistently supports. This
allowed MawjApp to position its suite around emotional recovery, mental centering,
sleep support, resonant flow, motivational readiness, and nap support with stronger
scientific clarity. When this kind of differentiation is used properly, it does more than
improve product language. It helps the brand become more credible.

NEURO EVALUATION OF
CHRISTMAS DRINK EXPERIENCE
Dunkin wanted to understand which festive cup design
creates the strongest emotional, sensory, and
repurchase response.

-- 41 of 75 --

The drink may stay the
same. The brain
experience does not.
In this study, the same Christmas drink
was served in four different cup
conditions:
plain cup, regular-branded cup, Christmas
red cup, and Christmas green cup.
What changed was not only how people
saw the drink, but how they felt it,
remembered it, and wanted to buy it
again.

-- 42 of 75 --

METHODOLOGY
We conducted a neuro study with 12 participants from Dunkin’s target audience. Each
participant sampled the same drink in four cup versions inside an isolated testing
setup. The full testing sequence lasted 2 minutes per version: 15 seconds observation,
15 seconds smell, and 90 seconds tasting.
SCIENTIFIC INSIGHT
The findings showed that design differences in the cup experience meaningfully
changed how the product was perceived, even when the drink itself remained the
same. Visual hierarchy, contrast, topping visibility, and festive cues all influenced
emotional engagement and memorability.
STRATEGIC OUTCOME
SparKitt translated these findings into practical recommendations across festive
packaging hierarchy, visual contrast and cup clarity, candy-cane ritual
communication, topping presentation, seasonal storytelling, and repurchase-focused
sensory cues. When festive design works properly, it does more than look seasonal. It
helps the brand become more noticeable, more emotionally engaging, more
memorable, and more likely to be chosen again.

Lebanon’s most iconic fast-food chain wanted to
validate one essential truth:
Does the product still win when the brand is stripped
away?

-- 44 of 75 --

METHODOLOGY
We conducted a blind taste test comparing Malak Al Tawouk’s signature sandwich
with those of key competitors. The process was entirely triple-blind; neither testers,
facilitators, nor analysts knew which brand was behind each sample. This ensured pure
sensory judgment, untainted by marketing or loyalty bias.
SCIENTIFIC INSIGHT
Surprisingly, a competitor’s sandwich was consistently chosen as the favorite across
test groups. While Malak Al Tawouk ranked high, it did not lead in blind preference. This
result was a critical insight: brand equity was strong, but product experience needed
evolution to match perception.
STRATEGIC OUTCOME
Rather than relying on legacy strength, Malak Al Tawouk gained clear, data-backed
direction for R&D and product innovation. SparKitt provided tailored
recommendations on flavor enhancement, texture optimization, and experience
design, all aimed at ensuring the product could match, and exceed, the emotional
power of the brand.
